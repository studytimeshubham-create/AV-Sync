AV-SYNC // ANTI-AI-SLOP DESIGN SPECIFICATION

AV-Sync was built from the ground up to deliberately reject the generic, cookie-cutter aesthetic of automated AI-generated websites (commonly dubbed "AI Slop").

1. The Anatomy of AI Slop in Web Design

Generative models and code scaffolds default to the statistical average of their training data. This leads to instantly recognizable cliches:

  • The Purple/Indigo Mesh Trap: Gratuitous gradients (purple-to-blue) on buttons, headers, and floating radial background blurs.
  • The "Inter / Roboto" Typography Monoculture: Neutral sans-serif fonts used across every element with no personality or hierarchy.
  • The Formulaic 3-Card Grid: A centered hero with an empty slogan ("Scale your potential") followed by 3 identical rounded-2xl cards with Lucide icons.
  • Fake Non-Functional Mockups: Decorative UI elements, static charts, and non-working dummy buttons.

2. Intentional Counter-Measures in AV-Sync
